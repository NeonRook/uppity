Organizations above their monitor limit now get 14 days before anything changes. Owners and admins are emailed when the organization goes over, with 7 days left, and with 1 day left, and the app shows a banner with the date. If the organization is still over its limit after that, Uppity pauses its newest monitors until it is back within the limit. Nothing is deleted. Paused monitors resume when you add capacity, or you can resume them yourself once there is room.

Self-hosted instances have no monitor limit and are unaffected.
