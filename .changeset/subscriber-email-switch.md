On the hosted service, staff now turn on status page email subscriptions per organization. Organizations with confirmed subscribers when they upgrade keep them, keep their subscribe form and keep sending. Other organizations no longer show the form until staff turn it on, and confirmation links already sent to their visitors stop working. Alerts are not affected.

Self-hosted instances need no action. Subscribe forms and subscriber email keep working for every organization, whether or not `SELF_HOSTED` is set.
