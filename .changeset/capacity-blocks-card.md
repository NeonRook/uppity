Uppity plan customers can add monitor capacity from Billing settings, in blocks of 50 monitors at $8 a month or $80 a year, up to 40 blocks. Removing blocks takes effect at the end of the billing period, since each period is billed at the most blocks held during it. Annual customers confirm an increase before it applies, with the amount they'll pay at renewal. Owners and admins can change capacity; other members see it read-only.
